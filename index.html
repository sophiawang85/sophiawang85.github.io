HOW A ROUND WORKS

Write. Draw. Match.

Each round starts with player-written prompts and ends with a gallery of everyone’s work.

  1. Write a promptContribute ideas to the shared pool.
  2. Draw someone else’s ideaRace against the clock to make it recognizable.
  3. Match art to titlesIdentify other players’ drawings and earn points.

Three rounds: standard drawing, invisible ink, and a final chaos round with a shared prompt pool.

BEHIND THE GAME

Some notes on
the backend.

Beyond getting players into the same room, the server had to hand out prompts fairly and keep everyone’s view of the game in sync.

01

Prompts without spoilers

I built server-side assignment logic that shuffles prompts and distributes them to other players. Matching excludes both the prompt’s author and the artist, then favors players with fewer assigned tasks.

Assignment logic ↗
02

Scores that reward both sides

Server-calculated scores combine how recognizable a player’s drawings are with their matching accuracy, applying different multipliers across rounds.

03

A shared sense of time

Socket.IO rooms carry phase changes, prompt counts, timers, and game-over events so players stay together through standard drawing, invisible ink, and the final chaos round.
